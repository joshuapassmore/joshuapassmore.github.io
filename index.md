Hello! I'm **Josh** (he/him), a Lecturer in Mathematics in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Sol Plaatje University (SPU)](https://www.spu.ac.za), and a pure mathematics PhD candidate at the [University of the Western Cape (UWC)](https://www.uwc.ac.za/). I joined SPU on 1 October 2026.

My research is focused mainly on pointfree topology, which I like to view as a lattice-based approach to general topology that also draws on ideas from category theory. My broader interests include topology, category theory, algebra, and logic. I completed my Master's in 2025; you can find my [thesis](https://uwcscholar.uwc.ac.za/items/74492698-1bbc-4858-b361-07f6ba42c2d3) online.

I am very passionate about higher education in the South African context, particularly in the mathematical sciences; I believe that education can empower individuals and communities, and strengthen unity in our society. I enjoy sharing ideas with, and learning from, others through teaching and collaborative research.

## Teaching Experience

I am of the opinion that mathematics undergraduate students (_including_ applied mathematics students) should have a course on set theory and logic as early as possible in their curricula. Set theory and propositional logic should be introduced together, ideally in the first year of one's studies, with emphasis on intuition rather than axioms — the axioms can always come later.

At AIMS South Africa, I worked as a Teaching Assistant on the following courses:

- **Model Theory — May 2026 and May 2025.** First-order logic, set theory, languages, structures, formulas, sentences, models, and the compactness theorem.
- **Algebraic Systems Biology — April–May 2026.** Biochemical reactions, Boolean and algebraic models of molecular networks, finite dynamical systems, and the dynamics of Boolean models.
- **Discrete Mathematics — March–April 2026.** Graph theory, the travelling salesperson and minimum weight spanning tree problems, and graphic and nongraphic matroids.
- **Mathematics and Art in Africa — March 2026.** Mathematical ideas in Ndebele house decorations, Shwe-Shwe fabric designs, Mozambican basketry, and Ghanaian Adinkra symbols; symmetries, dihedral groups, graph theory, Euler cycles, and elementary number theory.
- **Topology — February 2026.** Metric spaces, point-set topology, compactness, connectedness, and filter convergence.
- **Mathematical Problem Solving — January 2026 and October 2025.** Problems in number theory, combinatorics, probability, and geometry, together with set theory and methods of proof.
- **Verified Computation — November–December 2025.** Floating-point and interval arithmetic, verified linear algebra, rigorous evaluation of elementary functions, and verified equation solving.
- **Ordinary Differential Equations — November 2025.** Separable equations, changes of variables, integrating factors, and qualitative behaviour through trajectories, orbits, flows, and phase portraits.
- **Hilbert Spaces — June–July 2025.** Orthonormal bases, generalised Fourier series, functionals, and the Riesz representation theorem.

I also assisted with the Topology Honours module at UWC in 2023 and tutored mathematics and physics from 2019 to 2023.
