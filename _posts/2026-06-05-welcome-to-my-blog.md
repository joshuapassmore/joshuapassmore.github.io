# Hi!

I was born in Johannesburg, South Africa, and spent most of my childhood there; however, I have been living in Cape Town for the last eleven years. I completed my undergraduate at the [University of Cape Town](https://uct.ac.za/) in 2021 -- there I majored in pure mathematics and physics. In 2022 I signed up for an Honours degree at the [University of the Western Cape](www.uwc.ac.za) in pure mathematics -- this is where I met [Professor David Holgate](https://www.uwc.ac.za/news-and-announcements/news/prof-holgates-bold-vision-quality-impact-and-a-new-path-for-uwc-science); David introduced me to the world of general topology! At UWC, David leads the Topology for Tomorrow ("T4T") research group -- in 2023 I was fortunate enough to receive a T4T bursary which allowed me to pursue my Master's studies under the supervision of David.

During my Master's I focused on learning pointfree topology (viewing it as a blend of lattices and general topology); I subsequently decided to focus on the foundational topics in this area (you can view my thesis [here](https://uwcscholar.uwc.ac.za/items/74492698-1bbc-4858-b361-07f6ba42c2d3)).
