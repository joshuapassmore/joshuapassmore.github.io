# Hi!

I was born in Johannesburg, South Africa, and spent most my childhood there; however, I have been living in Cape Town for the last eleven years. I completed my undergraduate at the [University of Cape Town](https://uct.ac.za/) -- I majored in pure mathematics and physics. In 2022 I signed up for an Honours degree at the [University of the Western Cape](www.uwc.ac.za) in pure mathematics -- this is where I met [Professor David Holgate](https://www.uwc.ac.za/news-and-announcements/news/prof-holgates-bold-vision-quality-impact-and-a-new-path-for-uwc-science); David introduced me to the world of topology!
