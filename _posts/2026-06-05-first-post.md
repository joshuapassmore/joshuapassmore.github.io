# Hi!

I was born in Johannesburg, South Africa, and spent most of my childhood there; however, I have been living in Cape Town for the last eleven years. I completed my undergraduate at the [University of Cape Town](https://uct.ac.za/) in 2021 -- there I majored in pure mathematics and physics. In 2022 I signed up for an Honours degree at the [University of the Western Cape](www.uwc.ac.za) in pure mathematics -- this is where I met [Professor David Holgate](https://www.uwc.ac.za/news-and-announcements/news/prof-holgates-bold-vision-quality-impact-and-a-new-path-for-uwc-science); David introduced me to the world of general topology! At UWC, David leads the Topology for Tomorrow ("T4T") research group -- in 2023 I was fortunate enough to receive a T4T bursary which allowed me to pursue my Master's studies under the supervision of David.

During my Master's I focused on learning pointfree topology (viewing it as a blend of lattices and general topology); I subsequently decided to turn my attention to the foundational topics in this area (you can view my thesis [here](https://uwcscholar.uwc.ac.za/items/74492698-1bbc-4858-b361-07f6ba42c2d3)). In 2024 I became very interested in how one can "freely generate" frames over semilattices (or even sets); this is an example of an adjunction between the category of frames and the category of (meet-)semilattices. This adjunction in fact shows that the category of frames is **algebraic**. In 2025 I worked on the Kuratowski-Mrówka (KM) theorem for frames and locales, here I managed to prove the theorem without the Axiom of Choice (although I still used Excluded Middle) -- I believe the late Japie Vermeulen in fact gave a constructive proof! 

In February 2026 I signed up for my PhD at UWC -- continuing the tracjectory (for now) in pointfree topology -- at the moment I am working on coproducts of frames, and I want to be able to describe them in full! Perhaps a simpler description of the coproduct will yield a simpler proof of the localic KM theorem... More on this later!
