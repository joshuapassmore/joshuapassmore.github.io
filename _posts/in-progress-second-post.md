Many mathematical objects carry an inherent order within them. Probably the most familiar example is the set of natural numbers $\mathbb{N} = \{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. $\mathbb{N}$ forms a "chain" $0<1<2<\ldots<n<n+1<\ldots$ with least element $0$. --->
