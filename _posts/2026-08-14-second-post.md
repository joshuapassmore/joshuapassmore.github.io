description: "Every preordered set embeds universally into its complete lattice of downsets (with respect to all-join-preserving maps)"

Let $A$ be a preordered set. A subset $X \subseteq A$ is a *downset* iff whenever $x \in X$, $a \in A$, and $a \leq x$, it follows that $a \in X$. For example, given $a \in A$, the set ${\downarrow}a = \{x \in A \mid x \leq a \}$ is a downset -- it is the *principal downset generated by* $a$. Denote by $D(A)$ the poset of downsets of $A$, ordered by inclusion.
